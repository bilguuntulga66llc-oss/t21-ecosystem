Data

The financial datasets T21 provides, by category.

Reference Data

  • Securities and identifiers

    Production

    The securities listed on the exchange, with ISIN, exchange code and market identifier.

  • Company profiles

    Production

    Each listed company's profile as its exchange listing page states it.

Market Data

  • Daily equity prices

    Production

    One bar per security and trading day: open, high, low, close, volume, value and trade count.

  • Intraday prices

    Experimental

    The price, change and percentage change the source displays for each security during the session, captured every five minutes.

  • Market indices

    Production

    The exchange's index values and their daily history.

  • Market capitalisation

    Production

    The exchange's list of companies by market capitalisation.

  • Trades

    Production

    The trades of a trading session, including block trades.

Financial Statements

  • Financial statements as filed

    Production

    The statement of financial position, income statement, statement of changes in equity and cash flow statement of each filing, line by line as filed, by year and quarter.

Ownership & Governance

  • Major shareholders

    Production

    The major shareholders the exchange lists for each company, the date it states the list was updated, and the exchange's dated history of the list.

  • Board and officials

    Production

    Each company's board members and officials as the exchange lists them.

Corporate Actions & Disclosures

  • Company announcements

    Production

    Announcements companies publish through the exchange.

  • Dividends

    Production

    Declared dividends and the exchange's dividend register.

  • Issuer publications

    Limited

    Documents an issuer publishes on its own website, kept with their origin.

Macroeconomic Data

  • Official statistics

    Production

    A fixed set of the statistics office's tables, as received: prices, money, banking, exchange rates, securities, GDP, debt, budget and balance of payments.

Financial Analytics

  • Financial ratios

    Production

    Ratios T21 calculates from the filed statements: returns, margins, leverage, turnover and growth.

    Calculated by T21

  • Valuation history

    Production

    Market capitalisation, P/E, P/B and earnings per share for each trading day, calculated by T21.

    Calculated by T21

  • Ownership concentration

    Production

    The top holder's share, the top three and top five shares and the HHI, calculated by T21.

    Calculated by T21

  • Total return

    Experimental

    A holder's return over each session: the change in the price plus the dividend a share stopped carrying that session, calculated by T21.

    Calculated by T21

  • Statement reconciliation

    Production

    Checks T21 runs across a filing's statements, with each result and the units it compared.

    Calculated by T21

Risk Analytics

  • Risk measures

    Experimental

    Volatility, maximum drawdown, the largest daily move and robust return anomalies of the split-adjusted daily prices, calculated by T21.

    Calculated by T21

Research Data

  • Research catalog

    Experimental

    Features, labels and historical datasets for research.

    Compiled by T21
