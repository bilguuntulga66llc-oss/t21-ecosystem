Build with T21

The T21 API and its Python client give access to T21's financial data.

  1. Sign in to the Developer Platform with GitHub. Every signed-in account holds the open tier (open:read).
  2. Create a project. Its credential is created with it, and its secret is shown once.
  3. Exchange the credential for an access token and call the T21 API.

Two tiers

DataOpen tier
Securities and identifiers, company profiles, market capitalisation, announcements and corporate eventsAll
Daily prices, candles, trades, indices, dividends and the dividend registerThe last 90 days
Financial statementsEach issuer's latest statement
Ownership and governanceThe current state
Calculations, risk measures, valuation history, reconciliation, research dataNot included

The 90 days end on the newest stored trading session. Every response states the access it was served under in its access field, and a request for an earlier date is refused with 403 outside_open_access. The full history and the calculations are in the full tier, which T21 grants on request in the Developer Platform.

The National Statistics Office's tables need no credential (GET /v1/macro/statistics).

The concepts behind the data are in English; the full documentation is in Mongolian.
