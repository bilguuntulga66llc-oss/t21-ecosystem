Data quality

T21 serves data with its quality state. Missing, conflicting and uncertain data each arrive with their own state.

Price row states

StateMeaning
passThe row passed its checks.
no_tradeThe day's volume is 0. No trade took place, so the day has no traded price.
review_requiredNeeds review, for example a zero price on a day with trades.
unmeasuredThe history is empty; nothing was measured.

Missing and zero

  • An empty list, data never captured (never_captured), a source error (source_error) and data withheld by a reading each have their own state.
  • A value a statement states as 0 arrives as stated: 0. A cell that could not be read is recorded in checks.unreadable_items.
  • When a unit is not known (units_withheld), the monetary amounts it concerns are null.
