About T21

T21 is infrastructure for using financial data and calculations in software, research and algorithmic systems.

Purpose

To bring the collection and processing of financial data, its reconciliation with source documents, the links between its parts and its history into one shared infrastructure.

Technology

Financial objects
Companies, securities, exchange listings, financial statements, market observations, corporate events and ownership links are modelled as objects.
Data lineage
Each figure is recorded with its source, its source document, its unit and its version.
Historical state
When information applies, when it was published, and when it was observed and recorded are kept apart.
Data integrity
Data and financial calculations are checked for consistency, and missing or conflicting values are kept apart from stated figures.

Data and calculations

  • Capital markets: securities, trades, prices, indices and market capitalisation.
  • Company information: financial statements, shareholdings, boards of directors, officers, announcements and dividends.
  • Issuer publications: reports and documents issuers publish through their own channels.
  • Macroeconomy: selected statistical indicators.
  • Financial calculations: financial ratios and valuation measures.
  • Research: historical data, machine-learning datasets and analysis interfaces.
